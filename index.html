How to Use

  • Click any marker on the map to see district contact details and a quick popup.
  • Click "View All Schools" in the popup to open the detail drawer with phone, website, address, and quick actions.
  • Filter by region in the left sidebar to focus on a part of the state.
  • Search by district name, city, or area code to quickly locate a record.

Region Definitions

  • Wasatch Front — Salt Lake metro and surrounding urban districts (Alpine, Davis, Granite, Jordan, etc.)
  • Northern Utah — Cache Valley and northern border (Box Elder, Cache, Logan, Rich)
  • Central Utah — Sanpete, Sevier, Millard, Juab counties
  • Southern Utah — Washington, Iron, Kane, Garfield, Beaver counties
  • Eastern Utah — Uintah Basin and SE Utah (Duchesne, Uintah, Carbon, Emery, Grand, San Juan, Daggett)
  • Mountain Districts — High-altitude resort and rural mountain (Park City, Wasatch, North/South Summit, Morgan)
